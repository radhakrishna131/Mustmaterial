Quick Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Must Material really free?

Yes — always. There are no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no paywalls. All resources are free to access for every student.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Must Material requires no registration or sign-up. Access any resource instantly without sharing any personal information.

How do I upload study materials?

Visit our Upload page and submit your verified notes. Contributors earn recognition badges after peer review.

Can I request materials for a subject?

Absolutely! Use the contact form above and select "Study Material Request" — we'll do our best to add resources for your class.

How accurate are the materials?

All materials are peer-verified by students. We recommend cross-checking with your official textbooks for critical topics.

How can I report incorrect content?

Use the contact form with "Report a Bug / Issue" selected, or email us directly. We address content concerns promptly.

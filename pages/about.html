Our Story

Built by a Student,
For Every Student

Must Material started with a simple belief — quality education should be free, accessible, and built around what students actually need.

Who We Are

A platform born from real student struggles

Must Material was founded by Radha Krishna, a student who understood firsthand how difficult it can be to find reliable, organized study resources — especially for Indian curriculum boards and entrance exams.

What began as a personal collection of notes quickly grew into a full platform serving students across multiple classes, from 6th grade through B.Tech, covering EAPCET, board exams, and more.

Every resource on Must Material is curated, peer-verified, and made available completely free — no subscriptions, no paywalls, no sign-ups required.

What Drives Us

Our core values

Open Access

Every note, PDF and resource on this platform is free with no registration, no personal data, no barriers — just learning.

Peer Verified

Materials are reviewed and verified by fellow students to ensure accuracy and relevance for real exam preparation.

Student First

Every design decision, every resource, every feature — crafted specifically around the needs of students and their exam goals.

Community Driven

Students contribute their own notes and materials, earning recognition badges and helping thousands of peers succeed.

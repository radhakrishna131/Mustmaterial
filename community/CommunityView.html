Free Study Materials for AP Board Students

Must Material is a free educational platform that provides study notes, chapter summaries, model question papers and PDF resources for students following the Andhra Pradesh State Board curriculum. All resources on this platform are available at no cost and require no registration or subscription.

What kind of materials are available?

Must Material hosts a growing library of student-contributed resources covering multiple classes and subjects under the AP Board syllabus. These include chapter-wise notes written in clear, exam-focused language, previous year question papers with answer keys, subject-wise formula sheets and key points for quick revision, and model papers that reflect current AP Board exam patterns.

How are materials reviewed?

Every resource submitted to Must Material goes through a review process before it is published. Contributors are students and educators who are familiar with the AP Board curriculum. Materials that contain errors, are off-syllabus, or do not meet quality standards are not published. This ensures that students who rely on Must Material receive accurate and genuinely useful content.

Can I contribute my notes?

Yes. If you have handwritten or digital notes for any AP Board class, you can upload them here. Contributors receive recognition badges on the platform and help thousands of students who depend on Must Material for their exam preparation. All uploaded content is reviewed before it goes live.

Is Must Material affiliated with any coaching institute?

No. Must Material is an independent, student-run initiative and is not affiliated with any commercial coaching centre, publisher, or institution. It was built to give every AP Board student equal access to quality study resources, regardless of their financial background or location.

Contact and support

For questions about the platform, corrections to existing materials, or partnership enquiries, please visit the about page. Must Material is actively maintained and new resources are added regularly.

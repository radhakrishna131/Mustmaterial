About this section

Must Material is a free educational platform built to help students across Andhra Pradesh access reliable, exam-ready study materials without any barrier. This section of the website covers study resources for the AP State Board curriculum and is currently being populated with verified content.

Our content standards

Every note, question paper and formula sheet published on Must Material goes through a review process to ensure it is accurate, curriculum-aligned and genuinely useful for board exam preparation. We do not publish unverified or auto-generated content. Materials are contributed by students and educators who are familiar with the AP Board syllabus.
